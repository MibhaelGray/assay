# Standing lessons

- Don't take a post-earnings gap (held or faded) on the screen's numbers alone; name what will carry the reaction forward, and check what the reaction was about (a deal, a split, departures) rather than the beat or miss. The mechanical 11-09 gap trades went 3 for 9, mean about -307 bp (ABNB, CVS, U, RDW, UBER and CGNX lost; OUST, QCOM and NVO won).
- Don't take a stretched name with no turn as a bounce or a fade unless a dated event inside the window can turn it: the 70 the passes left alone, scored in the screen's direction, averaged -132 bp with 36% winners (to 2026-11-20; every night of one falling tape, so recheck when the tape changes).
- Don't short a breakdown that has no story of its own (the name fell with its sector or the tape): the 13 breakdowns the passes left alone, scored as shorts, averaged -402 bp with 15% winners (to 2026-11-20). The breakdown shorts worth taking had a company or macro reason that was still running.
