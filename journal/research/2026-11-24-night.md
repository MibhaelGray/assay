## Take

**1. Short USO (the fund that tracks WTI crude futures), outright, conviction 3, 4 sessions (through Monday 11-30), stop 7%.**
Crude fell 4.5% to its lowest close since August on the market's best day in a year, and through a weekend of attacks that should have lifted it (tankers in Hormuz, an Iranian attack on Bahrain, a claimed Houthi strike on Saudi Arabia's Rabigh refinery). What changed is the date: Trump told Fox News he may resume the war next year if talks fail, so the year-end escalation traders had hedged for moved out of the window. Behind it is a surplus: the IEA sees 1.7-2 million barrels a day this quarter, US crude stocks built in the last two reports, Chinese refinery runs are down 8.1% on the year, Libya restarted a pipeline, and pre-meeting reporting has OPEC+ lifting 2027 quotas. Outright because the belief is about crude and SPY moves against it.
Wrong if: the US or Israel strikes Iran, or the White House says the talks have failed or sets a new deadline; shipping through Hormuz is reported halted or sharply cut; the 11-25 EIA report shows a crude draw of 3 million barrels or more; or Reuters or Bloomberg reports OPEC+ preparing to cut or hold back 2027 output.
Against: down a fifth in a month and 3% above its 200-day (129.47); bank forecasts sit about at spot; one strike puts 5-8% on crude in a session, through the stop; the plan holds over the OPEC+ weekend.
