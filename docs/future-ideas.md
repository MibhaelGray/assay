## Twitter (X) timeline feed for the research agents

Added 2026-11-17 by the owner.

Scrape a Twitter timeline (accounts or lists still to be chosen) and feed what it finds to the research agents, so they see what traders and reporters are saying about a name, not only what has reached the web.

Questions to settle first:

- **Source.** Scraping X breaks its terms and is fragile. The paid API is the clean route, but it costs money and has rate limits.
- **What the agents get.** Today every agent gets the same bare brief and researches the open web on its own (the owner's rule, 2026-11-14). A feed changes that setup, so either it is offered as one more source the agent can search, or the change is made deliberately and the record notes the date it started.
- **Whose timeline.** A fixed list of accounts chosen by the owner, or a search by ticker.
- **Noise and manipulation.** Small-cap tickers attract pump posts. The research note should record when a take leans on a post, so the scoring can show whether those takes do better or worse.
- **Cost.** More text for every agent means a bigger share of the 5-hour limit per pass.
