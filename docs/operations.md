# Operations

How the project runs day to day: where the data comes from, every scheduled job, the headless research passes, and setting up the collector. Back to the [README](../README.md).

## Data and jobs

Data collection runs on the collector: a Mac that stays on, plugged in and logged in. It can be a second Mac or the main one; the nightly job stashes any edits in progress around its `git pull` and commits only the files it writes. The main Mac runs the research passes and records their verdicts.

Bars and earnings dates come from Yahoo Finance through `yfinance`, with no login. Option chains, and the borrow figures that arrive with them, come from IBKR through the live gateway. Since 2026-11-17 nothing collects them on a schedule (owner's call: the live gateway wasn't running and every run since 2026-11-05 had failed); the code is kept, so `fr options` still works by hand when the live gateway is up. Until it is run again, the brief's and the dashboard's option sections (dealer gamma, forced flows) show the last data, from 2026-11-05. Option data doesn't inform a trade decision. The IBKR connector, which doesn't need the gateway, supplies the night's market numbers, pre-market quotes and live marks. Web research feeds the research passes as context, not as stored data.

Since 2026-11-16 the live IBKR account subscribes to US market data (the US Securities Snapshot and Futures Value Bundle and the US Equity and Options Add-On Streaming Bundle, non-professional) and shares it with the paper account, so the paper gateway quotes stocks in regular hours over the API as well as outside them (before that, regular-hours quotes were refused with IBKR error 10089). The paper account borrows the live login's data, so logging in to the live account anywhere else (Client Portal in a browser, the mobile app, TWS) cuts the gateway's quotes off with error 10197. Log out there and restart the gateway (`docker restart deploy-ib-gateway-paper-1`); `fr paper enter` restarts it on its own when it sees that error.

Yahoo is unofficial and can miss a split on its first day. `fr bars` repairs that for ETFs (QUAL and MTUM on 2026-11-05) and lists any other move of 40% or more in the last five sessions as `suspect`, so read its output.

**The swing book (live since the night of 2026-11-15).** `fr paper restart --execute` archived the first book and the intraday book to `journal/archive/first-book-2026-11-15/` and started one swing book with $1,000,000 (`journal/paper/book.json`). Three research passes a day judge the live screen, and an order job trades what they record (the `swing-pass` skill; prompts in `deploy/passes/swing-*.md`; schedule from `python3 deploy/launchd/install.py --design three-pass`). The rules a pass works under are in the skill and PROJECT.md: it ranks every candidate (about 40 a pass since 2026-11-16) and every take at conviction 3 or more is entered, strongest first until the book's capital is in use (the best one to three until 2026-11-16), each with its own time limit (1-10 sessions) and stop (1-8%). Since 2026-11-16 each trade is sized by its risk (`rules.side_dollars`): dollars a side = the book's capital x 0.15% x (1 at conviction 3, 1.5 at 4, 2 at 5) / the stop, so every conviction-3 trade loses $1,500 of the $1,000,000 if stopped: $18,700 a side on an 8% stop, $30,000 on 5%, $50,000 on 3%. A side is never over 6% of capital or 0.25% of a leg's 20-day average dollar volume, and the trade keeps how it was sized under `sizing`. Until then every trade was $10,000 a side.

| `fr paper propose <draft>` | Mac, inside a pass | when the pass finishes | the pass's verdicts and ranks in `journal/paper/proposals/`, and its hold, exit or extend for each open trade in `journal/paper/pass_reviews/` |
| `fr paper enter` | collector | every minute | `journal/paper/entries/`: once a pass has recorded, its takes as orders that hit IBKR's bid or ask, and the exits it asked for. Nothing is left resting (since 2026-11-16): the order is a limit at the bid or ask, because IBKR's overnight venue takes nothing else, and whatever it didn't fill at once is cancelled before the run ends. No quote, or a spread over 2% on any leg: nothing is sent and the trade is passed on, never to enter (since 2026-11-18; until then it waited and retried every 15 minutes, and 11-18's Nike trade went in at 3:04 AM CT). Overnight, a leg the gateway sends nothing for while it quotes SPY has no overnight market and is passed on too (since 2026-11-18, HGV); any other missing quote is a gateway fault, so that trade still waits and retries. Since 2026-11-16 a trade's legs don't go out together: the leg hardest to fill (the one whose dollars are largest against its 20-day average dollar volume, kept as `lead`) is ordered in full, and each other leg only up to the share of the lead that has filled, a few seconds behind it in the same run and then every minute while the trade is part of the way in (`rules.lead_leg`, `rules.entry_due`). A top-up goes out once it is a tenth of its leg or completes it, and a leg that can't be sent holds the lead back too. Before that, both legs went at once, and on the night of 2026-11-16 the ARKK hedge of a Red Cat short filled in seconds while the short took ninety minutes. In the regular session it also checks every open trade against its stop every two minutes (`entries.STOP_EVERY`, since 2026-11-18), on the same Yahoo latest-minute prices the close job uses, and a trade past it exits at once, all legs together at the bid or ask; a stop exit with no gateway quote isn't sent, is tried again at the next check, and otherwise goes into the closing auction. Until then stops were checked only at 2:45 PM CT, and HubSpot was 8.4% down against a 7% stop when it was caught |
| `fr watch` | collector | every 5 minutes | `data/watch.json`: the watchdog (since 2026-11-17). A second IBKR username for the phone avoids it for good. The same alert isn't repeated for 3 hours (a job's for a day). Alerts never carry account numbers, positions or dollars. `fr watch --test` sends a test alert. Separately, each scheduled pass sends "started", "recorded" or "FAILED" (passes.py), and the order job sends "<pass> pass traded" once it has acted on a pass's takes, in counts only: how many went in, partly in, were passed on for a wide spread, or wait (`entries.traded_note`, since 2026-11-18) |

| `fr paper sync` | collector | 3:20 PM | `journal/paper/fills/`: the day's fills, commissions, positions and account value |

The night pass skips Friday nights; the morning and midday passes run only on trading days. A by-hand order labelled `cleanup-<trade id>` (no `|SYMBOL`) is counted against that trade in the fill's own symbol, as the CTVA cover of 2026-11-16 was, so a sync that rewrites the day's fills can't reopen it.

The dashboard page lives in `dashboard/page.html`, the same file published to claude.ai as the [Factor Rotation Ledger](https://claude.ai/artifact/TVknCZmSXjToEPbmMGPSKv). The Refresh button only appears under `fr serve`; the claude.ai page can't run a pull. A refresh during the session stores a partial bar for the day, so the screen and the proposals' plan results wait for a session until its bars have been pulled after 4:15 PM ET. `fr dashboard` wraps it with `dashboard/shim.js` (a stand-in for the claude.ai runtime) and `dashboard/tokens.css`, and embeds tonight's market rows, so it opens from disk with no server. Tonight's market rows need the option data in `data/`, so on a machine without it that section shows as failed. Charts load d3 from a CDN.

Market data lives on the collector's disk under `FR_DATA_ROOT` (default `data/` in the repo). The journal (briefs, scans, screens and the paper book) travels through GitHub: the nightly job commits and pushes what it wrote and what the day's research passes recorded.

## Research passes outside the Claude app

Since 2026-11-16 the swing book's three passes run as launchd jobs, not as Claude app tasks: `deploy/run.sh pass-night` (and `pass-morning`, `pass-midday`) runs `fr pass <name>` (`passes.py`), which starts Claude Code headless with the pass's prompt from `deploy/passes/`. A headless run pins the models, which the app can't: the judge runs on Fable 5.2 at high effort (`--model claude-fable-5-2 --effort high`) and every research agent on Sonnet 5.5 at medium effort (`CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_MODEL=claude-sonnet-5-5`, and the prompt tells it to spawn agents with `model: "sonnet"`, `effort: "medium"`; Opus 5.5 until 2026-11-16). A headless run gets 200 web searches by default, shared by all its agents, which ran out on 2026-11-16; `passes.py` raises it to 2,500 with `CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_WEB_SEARCHES_PER_SESSION`; `fr paper depth` checks both in the record. It signs in with `FR_CLAUDE_TOKEN` from `.env` (from `claude setup-token`) and finds the Claude Code installed by the desktop app.

The run uses `--permission-mode dontAsk` with an allowlist: it can read files, search the web, spawn research agents, write its draft to `data/pass-drafts/`, write its research note under `journal/research/` and edit `journal/paper/lessons.md`, and run only `fr screen --live <pass>`, `fr paper propose` (its own draft), `fr paper postmortem`, `fr paper depth`, `fr paper hedges` and `fr paper status`. Everything else is refused. It has no IBKR connector, so market numbers come from `uv run fr quotes SPY QQQ IWM ^VIX ^TNX CL=F ES=F NQ=F` (Yahoo). A pass has 150 minutes. Each run's result (turns, agents, minutes, anything refused, the final message) is in `data/pass-logs/<session>-<pass>.json` and its output in `~/Library/Logs/factor-rotation/pass-<name>.log`.

`fr pass late` is a fourth pass, run by hand only (since 2026-11-16): a second pass in the night window, for the same session as the night pass. It screens with `fr screen --live late`, leaves out the names the night pass judged, records as `"pass": "late"` and is traded by `fr paper enter` like any other pass. It writes no post-mortems or lessons. It can be run again the same night: each further run records as `late2`, `late3` or `late4`.

`fr pass <name> --shadow` runs a pass on a copy of the journal in `data/shadow-journal` with `FR_PAPER_SEND=0`, so it records and trades nothing that counts: use it to try a prompt change.

## Collector setup (macOS)

1. **IBKR login:** the account must be on IBKR Pro (Lite accounts can't use the API). Create a second username in Client Portal (Settings → Users & Access Rights) and subscribe it to US options (OPRA) and US equity market data. Its own subscriptions mean your own TWS or phone sessions never cut off the collector. Expect an IB Key approval on your phone about once a week, when the gateway's session expires.
2. **Software:** install Docker Desktop (Settings → General → Start Docker Desktop when you sign in), `uv` and git. Clone this repo, set `git config user.name` and `user.email` in it, and give the Mac an SSH key with write access to the GitHub repo and no passphrase, so the nightly job can push unattended.
3. **Settings:** copy `.env.example` to `.env` and fill in the login.
4. **Start the gateway:** `docker compose -f deploy/docker-compose.yml up -d`. It restarts with Docker Desktop.
5. **Keep it awake on power.** Jobs that come due while the Mac sleeps run late, when it wakes, so the snapshots would be mistimed. In System Settings, turn on automatic login, and under Battery → Options turn on "Prevent automatic sleeping on power adapter when the display is off". Or from a terminal:
   ```bash
   sudo pmset -c sleep 0 disksleep 0
   ```
   Leave the lid open, or closed with an external display attached; a closed lid without one sleeps regardless. Each job also runs under `caffeinate`, so the Mac stays awake until it finishes.
6. **Install the schedules:** `python3 deploy/launchd/install.py`. It converts the New York schedule to the Mac's time zone (rerun it if that changes) and installs one launchd agent per job. `--dry-run` shows the plists without installing, `--uninstall` removes them.
7. **First run:** `uv run fr bars` (backfills 5 years of bars, no gateway needed), then `uv run fr options --symbols SPY,QQQ` to check permissions and timing.

Logs: `~/Library/Logs/factor-rotation/<job>.log`. To run a job now: `launchctl kickstart gui/$(id -u)/com.factor-rotation.nightly`.


## Moving the collector to another Mac

1. Set up the new Mac with steps 1–6 above, but don't start the gateway yet.
2. On the old one: `python3 deploy/launchd/install.py --uninstall`, then `docker compose -f deploy/docker-compose.yml down`. One gateway per username, so stop this one first.
3. Copy the market data across (Remote Login on, on the new Mac): `rsync -az data/ newmac.local:<repo path>/data/`. Past option chains can't be re-downloaded, so check the file counts match before deleting anything.
4. On the new Mac: start the gateway, install the schedules and kickstart `bars` to check it connects.

## Backup

The collector's disk holds the only copy of the market data, and past option chains can't be re-downloaded. Copy it to the main Mac now and then (turn on System Settings → General → Sharing → Remote Login on the collector first):

```bash
rsync -az collector.local:<repo path>/data/ ~/Documents/"ETF Factor Rotation"/data/
```
